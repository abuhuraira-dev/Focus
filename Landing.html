The cost of switching

Missed details cost more than time.

A missed deadline can strain a client relationship. An invoice you forget to follow up on can leave completed work unpaid.

01 / TIME

Deadlines slip.

Requests arrive across conversations. Without a clear next step and delivery date, important work gets buried.

02 / INCOME

Payments get overlooked.

You finish the work, then move to the next client. Invoice status and payment follow-ups need a place too.

03 / FOLLOW-THROUGH

Small tasks pile up.

Approvals, revisions, and handoffs compete with delivery. Keeping them in your head makes it easier to miss one.

Before you start

A few practical details.

Where is my data saved?

In this browser on this device. Export backups regularly. Clearing browser data can remove your workspace. There is no cloud sync.

Will Focus remind me when the app is closed?

No. This version offers check-ins inside the app. Background reminders are not available, and the focus timer requires the page to stay open.

Does Focus send invoices or collect payments?

No. Payment tracking is manual. You can add your own client projects, deadlines, invoice records, and tasks. This personal MVP also includes the owner’s business projects and practice routines. Accounts, billing, and client portals are not available.
